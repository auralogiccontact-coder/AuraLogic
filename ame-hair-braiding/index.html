How much will my style cost?
It depends on size, length and hair type. Send a request with an inspo photo and Ame will text you a quote.

        
How long does it take?
From about an hour for simple cornrows to most of the day for small knotless. You'll get a time estimate with your quote.

        
Can I bring my own hair?
Mention it in your request and Ame will tell you how to prep when she confirms.

        
Do you braid kids' hair?
Kids' styles are on the menu. Put the child's age and the style in your notes so enough time is set aside.
