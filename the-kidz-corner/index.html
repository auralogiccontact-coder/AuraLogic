About The Kidz Corner

Fifteen years of caring for little ones.

The Kidz Corner is a NYS licensed daycare & preschool bringing 15 years of hands-on childcare experience to Holbrook and the surrounding community. We're opening our doors on September 3rd, and we can't wait to welcome your family.

Our story

Built on 15 years in the daycare field.

The Kidz Corner was created around one belief: that a daycare should feel like an extension of home. With 15 years of experience in the daycare field, our team has spent years learning how young children grow, play, and thrive — and we've brought all of that into one warm, welcoming space.

We're a NYS licensed daycare & preschool with certified staff, offering care for children ages 6 weeks through 4 years old. Whether your family needs full-time or part-time care, our goal is the same: a program where your child feels safe, seen, and excited to come back each day.

Our approach

Every child grows at their own pace.

From our infant room through preschool, our programs are built around each child's age and stage — gentle, individual care for the youngest babies, and guided play and early learning as children grow toward kindergarten readiness.

Our Programs

Care & learning for every age.

From infant care through preschool, every program at The Kidz Corner is built around your child's age and stage — with full-time and part-time options to fit your family.

6 weeks – 18 months

Infant Services

Gentle, one-on-one attention for our youngest friends. We follow each baby's own feeding, sleeping, and play routine in a calm, nurturing environment designed just for infants.

18 months – 3 years

Toddler Services

Active, hands-on days full of movement, exploration, and early language and social skills, as toddlers build confidence and take their first steps toward independence.

3 – 4 years

Preschool

Guided play and early learning activities that build curiosity, focus, and school readiness — getting little ones excited and prepared for kindergarten.

Full-time & part-time options available

We work with families to find the schedule that fits — contact us for current openings and tuition details for your child's age group.
