01Who we are

AuraLogic (“we,” “us,” or “our”) designs and builds websites for small businesses. We’re based in Riverdale, Georgia. This policy explains what information we collect through auralogicwebsite.com and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

02Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly when you request a free mockup, send a message, unlock pricing, place an order or complete our onboarding form. Depending on the form, this may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your business name, type, address or service area, and business phone and email
  • Your social media handles (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) and current website, if any
  • Business details you choose to share, such as your services and prices, hours, brand colors, logo, photos and booking system
  • Your preferred way for us to contact you

We also collect basic website activity automatically through the Meta Pixel, such as which pages you visit and which ads brought you here (see section 4).

03How we use your information

  • To build your free mockup and the website you order
  • To contact you about your request, project, payment or support
  • To process orders and keep records of what you purchased
  • To understand which pages and ads are working, so we can improve our marketing
  • To show relevant AuraLogic ads on Facebook and Instagram

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with other businesses for their own marketing.

04Meta Pixel and advertising

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram). It uses cookies and similar technology to record visits and actions on our site so we can measure our ads and show AuraLogic ads to people who may be interested. Meta’s use of this data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

You can control ad personalization in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings, and you can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.

05Services we use

We rely on a few trusted providers to run our website and business. They receive only the information needed to do their job:

  • Stripe processes payments. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Formspree delivers the forms you submit on our site to our inbox.
  • Cal.com handles booking if you schedule a call with us.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on our site, which means your browser connects to Google when pages load.
  • Meta provides the Meta Pixel described above.

06Texts, calls and emails

If you give us your phone number or email, we may contact you about your mockup, project, order or support request. If you choose text messages as your preferred contact method, we’ll text you about your project. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop texts, or simply tell us. We don’t send marketing texts without your permission.

07How long we keep it

We keep project and order information for as long as we’re working together and as needed for our business records, taxes and any legal obligations. If you request a mockup but don’t become a client, you can ask us to delete your information at any time.

08Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • See the personal information we have about you
  • Correct anything that’s wrong
  • Delete your information, unless we need to keep it for legal or record-keeping reasons
  • Stop contacting you

Email us using the details below and we’ll respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state’s privacy laws.

09Security

We use reputable providers and take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or online service is 100% secure, so please don’t send sensitive information like passwords or card numbers through our forms or by email.

10Children

Our website and services are for business owners and are not directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted on our website.
