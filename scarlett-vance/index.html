About Scarlett

Wealth planning shouldn’t feel like a product being sold to you — it should feel like a plan being built with you.

Scarlett Vance is a Senior Wealth Advisor at Meridian Capital Partners, where she works closely with a focused group of professionals and business owners who are navigating major financial transitions — selling a practice, approaching retirement, or structuring wealth to pass on to the next generation.

Her approach is unhurried and detail-driven: understand the full picture first — income, tax exposure, timeline, family goals — then build a plan built to hold up over decades, not just the next market cycle.

How We Work Together

A clear process, in the order it actually happens.

01

Discover

A no-pressure conversation about where things stand today — income, assets, obligations, and what you actually want the next chapter to look like.

02

Design

Scarlett builds a plan specific to your timeline and goals — covering retirement income, tax strategy, investments, and estate considerations.

03

Implement

The plan gets put into motion, coordinated with your CPA and attorney where needed, so nothing falls through the cracks.

04

Review

Regular check-ins to adjust the plan as life, markets, and tax law change — wealth planning is never really “done.”
