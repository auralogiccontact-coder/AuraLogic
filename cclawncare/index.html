Run By Barbers Who Actually Listen

Good News Barbershop opened its doors on South Main Street with one goal: give Jonesboro a shop where the fade is always clean, the wait doesn't drag, and every barber treats your cut like it matters — because it does.

Regulars keep coming back for the same reasons: barbers who take their time, a shop that stays spotless, and a straightforward answer when you ask "what do you think would look good?"
