Our Story

Sparrow's Nest, on the Poospatuck Indian Reservation

Alyssa "Sparrow" Treadwell is a member of the Unkechaug Nation, running Sparrow's Nest from the Poospatuck Indian Reservation in Mastic, Long Island. Every crystal, pendant, and smudge bundle on these shelves is chosen with care — a shop built on healing, family, and living Indigenous culture, not just retail.

Sparrow's Nest carries forward a family tradition rooted in the Unkechaug community, and welcomes every visitor — whether they walk through the door or shop from across the country — to take part in that spirit.

We walk three paths — modern life, faith, and the old ways of our ancestors. One goal stays the same: to hold onto who we are. — A teaching passed down through the Treadwell family, Unkechaug Nation
