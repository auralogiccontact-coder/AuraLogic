    Questions, answered.
    
  
    How is my price set?
    By the type of clean, your bedrooms and bathrooms, home size and how often you book. The estimator gives a close range; Ashley confirms the final price by text or a quick walk-through before work starts.
    
  
    Do I need to be home?
    No. Many clients share a door code or lockbox. Just add access notes to your request and Ashley will confirm them with you.
    
  
    Do you bring your own supplies?
    Yes. If you prefer a specific product — or have allergies or pets — note it in your request and we'll use what you like.
    
  
    Do you clean businesses and after events?
    Yes — offices and commercial spaces, after-party and wedding clean-ups, and new-construction clean-ups. These are quoted after a quick look at the space.
    
  
    Do you offer pressure washing?
    Yes. Driveways, walkways, patios and more — so the outside shines like the inside. Request a quote and include photos if you can.
    
  
    What are your hours?
    Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. Text anytime and Ashley will get back to you during business hours.
