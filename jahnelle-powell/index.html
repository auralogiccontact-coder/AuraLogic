About Jahnelle

Buying or selling a home is one of the biggest decisions you’ll make — it deserves an agent who treats it that way.

Jahnelle Powell is a Real Estate Salesperson with Compass Real Estate & Homes, serving buyers and sellers across Suffolk, Nassau, and New York City. She holds her Accredited Buyer’s Representative (ABR) and Seller Representative Specialist (SRS) designations — specialized training focused on protecting your interests on both sides of the table.

Whether it’s your first home or your next one, Jahnelle brings sharp local market knowledge, steady communication, and genuine care to every step of the process.
