Our Story

Care That Feels Like Family

Amelia's Hands Home Care Services was built on a simple belief: everyone deserves to age and heal in the comfort of their own home, surrounded by dignity and genuine care.

Led by CEO Nikita Farley-Thompson, our team works closely with every family to build a care plan around real needs — not a one-size-fits-all package. We treat every client the way we'd want our own family treated.

How We Help

Our Home Care Services

Every care plan is built around the individual — here's a look at the support we provide most often.

Personal Care

Bathing, dressing, grooming, and mobility assistance delivered with patience and respect.

Companion Care

Friendly companionship, conversation, and shared activities to fight isolation and lift spirits.

Homemaking & Light Housekeeping

Meal prep, laundry, tidying, and errands so the household keeps running smoothly.

Respite Care

Short-term relief for family caregivers, so you can rest knowing your loved one is in good hands.

Medication Reminders

Gentle reminders and routine support to help keep health plans on track.

Transportation

Safe rides to appointments, errands, and visits — so no one misses what matters.
