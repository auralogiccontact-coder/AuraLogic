01What do I need to send you?

One intake form, which takes about 5–7 minutes. It covers your services and prices, business hours, address or service area, brand colors, and how you take bookings now. Then email us your logo and a few photos of your work or your space. No logo yet? Just say so on the form and we’ll talk through options.

02Do I own it?

Yes. You pay once and the website is yours. There’s no required monthly fee and no contract keeping you with us. And unlike an Instagram page or a booking app, it isn’t rented space that can be restricted overnight.

03What does the 48-hour clock actually start from?

It starts once your intake is confirmed: you’ve paid, sent your intake form, and we’ve checked that we have everything we need. We review every intake within 24 hours. From that confirmation, your site is designed, built and live within 48 hours. If something’s missing, like your logo or prices, the clock starts when it arrives.

04What if I don’t like it?

You don’t have to guess before you pay. Request a free mockup first and see exactly what we’d build for your business. After launch you get 30 days of free edits to get it right, whether that’s wording, photos or colors.

05Do you work outside Georgia?

Yes, anywhere in the U.S. We’re based in Riverdale, GA, but everything happens online, so we can build for your business wherever you are.

06What happens after 30 days?

Your site is still yours. For changes after that, you have two options: pay $25 per update whenever you need one, or add the Care Plan for $50/month, which covers hosting, unlimited small edits and a monthly Google Business post. Cancel anytime.
