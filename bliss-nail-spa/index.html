The Salon

A Hapeville Favorite Since 2022

Bliss Nail Spa is a full-service nail salon on N Central Ave, known for clean, contemporary interiors and a team that treats every set like it matters. Regulars ask for their techs by name — Lee, Q, and Chu are among the favorites, each bringing their own eye for detail and design.

Whether you're after a simple, healthy manicure or a fully custom themed set, Bliss is built to be a full destination — nails, pedicures, and waxing, all under one roof.
