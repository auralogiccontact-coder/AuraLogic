Our Story

MORE THAN A
HAIRCUT.

At Kleen KUT, we don't just cut hair — we build confidence. Located on Tara Boulevard in the heart of Jonesboro, our shop is where the community comes to look and feel their best.

  • Master-Level Barbers
    Every barber in our shop is a certified craftsman — years of experience behind every blade.
  • Walk-Ins Always Welcome
    No appointment needed. Pull up, sit down, and get in the chair — every day of the week.
  • Special Needs Friendly
    Our barbers are patient, kind, and experienced working with clients of all abilities.
  • Open 7 Days a Week
    10 AM – 7 PM, Monday through Sunday. We're here when you need us.
