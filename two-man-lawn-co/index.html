The Problem

The truck doesn't have Bluetooth for a reason.

When you're behind a mower, you're not reaching for your phone. So the call goes to voicemail — and most people don't leave one. They just call the next name on the list.

1 truck

No VM

Same day

People calling a lawn crew usually want an answer today, not tomorrow.

The Fix

Every missed call gets a reply — automatically.

No new phone, no new number, no app to check between jobs. The second a call is missed, a text goes out on its own.
