About the Kween

Nia Kennedy

My name is Nia Kennedy and I'm originally from Tallahassee, FL. Today I do most of my creative art work from Atlanta, GA, and sign my work under the artist name "Kween K."

I find inspiration in many places, and traveling to different areas of Atlanta has shaped my creative style and process. I create art because it's a way to express myself — and because I want people to understand that there is beauty in abstract lines, colors, and shapes. I specialize in acrylic paint and focus on a range of subject matter, and I also love working with film photography.

My art style is abstract expressionism. I'm motivated by my daughter, and by the artists who shaped how I see color and freedom on a canvas — Renee Stout, Joan Mitchell, Jackson Pollock, and Astrid Svangren are my biggest inspirations, with Helen Frankenthaler as a personal favorite for work that's truly out of the box. I've been creating for years, but finally took myself seriously as an artist two years ago.

In the Community

Beyond the Canvas

I connect with the art community by teaching visual arts to elementary school kids — helping the next generation find their own freedom of expression, one lesson at a time. My work has also been shown at several art shows across Atlanta, and I'm always looking for new spaces to share it.

I intend for others to experience the same freedom of expression when they see my work that I feel when I make it.
